===========================================================================
Summary
===========================================================================

This guide explained how clinical and public health laboratories could approach validation of bioinformatics pipelines within an ISO 15189 context. 

The guide began by describing why genomics is now embedded across clinical and public health practice, why bioinformatics pipelines are multi-component and complex, and why validating them is harder than validating a conventional wet laboratory method. It set out the principles of validation under ISO 15189, including fitness for intended use, assessment against predefined performance characteristics, the requirement for validation/validation before clinical use, and ongoing monitoring and revalidation.  

Following this guide should enable laboratories to develop a validation plan for their bioinformatics pipelines, and to generate the evidence required to demonstrate that the pipelines is fit for its intended clinical use.
